An extremely lightweight World of Warcraft addon that displays a customizable on-screen warning message and plays an audio alert as soon as your health drops to a critical level (below 35%).

*❤️‍🔥 Never get caught off guard by low health again! ❤️‍🔥*

---

## 🚀 Features

- **Customizable Visual Alert:** Displays a prominent warning message in the center of your screen with full control over custom text, font family (7 built-in styles), font size, and RGBA color & opacity.
- **Loud & Clear Audio Cues:** Choose from over 35 built-in and custom notification sounds played directly on the `Master` audio channel—complete with smart overlap protection so alerts never spam.
- **Live Preview & Testing:** Includes an interactive preview box in the settings panel and a **Test Alert** button so you can fine-tune your look and sound outside of combat.
- **Hyper-Lightweight:** Built from the ground up to be future-proof and highly optimized, ensuring zero impact on your framerate (FPS).
- **Cross-Flavor Support:** Fully compatible with **Retail**, **Classic**, and **Forever** flavors of World of Warcraft.

## ⚙️ Configuration & Commands

You can access the configuration options to customize your warning text, color, font, and alert sound directly via the game interface:

- **In-Game Menu:** Press `Esc` ➡️ `Options` ➡️ `AddOns` ➡️ **LowHP**

## 📦 Installation

To ensure you always have the latest, verified, and secure version of LowHP, all downloads are hosted exclusively on CurseForge.

### Option 1: Automatic Update (Recommended)
The easiest way to install and keep LowHP updated automatically is through the CurseForge App:
👉 **[Download LowHP on CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/low-hp)**

### Option 2: Manual Installation
If you prefer to install your addons manually, please download the official release package directly from CurseForge to ensure you get the latest stable build:
1. Go to the **[LowHP CurseForge Page](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/low-hp)** and click **Download**.
2. Extract the contents of the downloaded `.zip` archive.
3. Move the extracted `LowHP` folder into your World of Warcraft AddOns directory:
   * **Retail:** `World of Warcraft/_retail_/Interface/AddOns/`
   * **Classic:** `World of Warcraft/_classic_/Interface/AddOns/`
   * **Classic Era:** `World of Warcraft/_classic_era_/Interface/AddOns/`
4. Restart or reload the game (`/reload`).
